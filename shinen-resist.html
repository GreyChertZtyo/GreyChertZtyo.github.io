Scene direction and script lead on a mobile RPG: led four scripters and planned new script engine features.

Role
Script Lead
Team
5 (led 4 scripters)
Period
2017-12 — 2019-01
Engine
Unity; proprietary script engine
Tools

Official promotional material illustrates the game; it does not identify individual scene authorship.

Challenge

Scene direction on a live mobile RPG where, from March 2018, four scripters had to be directed and new script engine features specified alongside running production. Progress was presented at regular meetings, so the schedule had to hold.

What I did

  • Directed scenes through design, implementation and live operation.
  • Led four scripters as script lead from March 2018.
  • Planned new features for the script engine.
  • Managed the team members' schedules.
  • Controlled the quality of the scene direction.
  • Presented progress at regular meetings, preparing materials in PowerPoint and Word.

Result

Held scene direction and scripting leadership from December 2017 to January 2019, leading four scripters from March 2018.
