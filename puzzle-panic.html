Puzzle Panic

Lead Planner on a live-ops mobile match-3: episodes, timed events, new obstacles, competitor research and playable HTML5 event prototypes.

Role
Lead Game Planner
Team
Approximately 70 people
Period
Feb 2025 – Present
Engine
Unity; HTML5 for prototypes; Google Spreadsheets
Tools
HTML5, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Claude Code, Codex, Gemini

Official promotional material illustrates the game; it does not identify individual scene authorship.

Challenge

A live-ops match-3 shipping episodes and time-limited events on a rolling schedule, with about 70 people on the project. Every new mechanic needed its own specification, a working prototype and a presentation to management, and then had to be integrated into the Unity build without disturbing the existing puzzle balance.

What I did

  • Wrote GDDs for episodes and time-limited events, including the new mechanics behind them.
  • Designed new puzzles and obstacles and rebalanced the existing puzzle set.
  • Analysed competing titles and researched which mechanics actually work.
  • Prototyped events in HTML5 and presented them to management.
  • Integrated assets, built scenes, implemented animation, verified triggers, debugged and automated routine work in Unity.
  • Coordinated the UI team and supported the producer in working with the UA and sales art teams.
  • Interpreted all meetings in Japanese, English and Russian, translated texts EN↔JP and handled Russian localization.

Result

Every event reached management as a playable HTML5 prototype rather than a document. The project has been in live operation since February 2025; the Android version is built but not yet released.
