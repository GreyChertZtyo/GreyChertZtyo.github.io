BUSTAFELLOWS

Scene direction for the iOS and Android version of BUSTAFELLOWS: ADV cutscenes and mystery-solving scenes.

Role
Game Planner / Scene Direction
Team
Undisclosed (Indirect Contract)
Period
2019-01 — 2019-02
Engine
Unity; proprietary tools
Tools
In-house script language, Excel, Word, PowerPoint

Official promotional material illustrates the game; it does not identify individual scene authorship.

Challenge

The mystery scenes and ADV cutscenes are what the reading experience rests on, so the staging had to be right. The work covered design, implementation, testing and live operation in a team of five.

What I did

  • Staged scenes for story sequences as the scene director of the mobile version.
  • Developed the mystery-solving scenes.
  • Developed ADV cutscenes in Unity with the in-house script language.
  • Carried scene direction through design, implementation, testing and live operation.

Result

Delivered the scene direction of the iOS and Android version from design through live operation.
