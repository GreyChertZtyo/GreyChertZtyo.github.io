Pokémon LEGENDS Z-A

Planner on the Story Team: cameras and cutscenes for every side quest and 15–20% of the main story.

Role
Planner (Story Team)
Team
Story Team: 10 people; 925 people in the credits
Period
2023-10 — 2024-12
Engine
Internal 3D engine; Nintendo Switch / Switch 2 SDK
Tools
Proprietary tools, Nintendo Switch / Switch 2, Excel, Word, PowerPoint

Official promotional material illustrates the game; it does not identify individual scene authorship.

Challenge

A large RPG production with more than 30 people on the project and a ten-person Story Team inside the Planning Section. Every side quest needed its own cameras and staging while staying consistent with the direction set for the rest of the game.

What I did

  • Created cameras and cutscenes for every side quest in the game.
  • Created cameras and cutscenes for 15–20% of the main story.
  • Took part in creating side quests from the initial idea through to implementation.
  • Designed the placement of NPCs and Pokémon in the game world.
  • Worked through implementation and testing with proprietary tools on Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 hardware.

Result

Credited by name in the Story Team of the Planning Section; the end credits list 925 people.
