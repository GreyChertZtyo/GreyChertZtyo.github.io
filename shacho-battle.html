Shacho, Battle no Jikan desu!

Feature specs, master data, stage themes and balance of features, characters, skills and equipment for a mobile RPG.

Role
Game Planner
Team
4
Period
2017-04 — 2017-12, 2019
Engine
Unity; Excel; Google Spreadsheets; Confluence
Tools
Google Sheets, Confluence, Excel, Word, PowerPoint

Official promotional material illustrates the game; it does not identify individual scene authorship.

Challenge

Two stints on the same mobile RPG: new feature design in 2017 and stage and combat design in 2019. Specifications, master data and balance were owned by the same person in both periods.

What I did

  • Wrote feature specifications, both overview and detailed.
  • Created character and event master data.
  • Wrote ADV-part scripts and staged them.
  • Drafted animation plans for staged sequences.
  • Balanced new features, character data and event data.
  • Planned stage themes and tuned enemy parameters and placement to match them.
  • Balanced skills and equipment.
  • Managed the team schedule and debugged builds.

Result

Delivered feature specifications, master data and combat balance for the same title across the 2017 and 2019 stints.
